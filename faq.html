Common Questions

Renovation FAQ

Everything you need to know about working with Building Renovation Company.

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Toronto?
+

Kitchen renovations in Toronto range from $15,000 to $65,000 depending on size, materials, and scope. A simple refresh with new cabinets and countertops starts around $15,000. A full gut renovation with custom millwork, quartz countertops, and new appliances can reach $65,000+. We provide free, detailed written estimates.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?
+

A typical bathroom renovation takes 2–4 weeks. A simple refresh (new tile, vanity, fixtures) can be done in 1–2 weeks. A full gut renovation with custom tile and heated floors usually takes 3–4 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline before starting.

How much does basement finishing cost in Toronto?
+

Basement finishing in Toronto typically ranges from $35,000 to $90,000 depending on size and finishes. An open concept rec room with a bathroom starts around $35,000. A fully finished in-law suite with a kitchen and separate entrance can reach $90,000+.

Do you handle permits?
+

Yes — we handle all permit applications on your behalf for projects that require them. This includes structural changes, electrical, plumbing rough-in, and basement egress windows. Permit costs are included in your estimate.

Do you provide free estimates?
+

Yes, absolutely. We offer free, no-obligation estimates. We visit your home, assess the scope of work, and provide a detailed written quote within 48 hours. There is no cost and no commitment required.

Are you licensed and insured?
+

Yes. We are fully licensed and carry comprehensive liability insurance and WSIB coverage. All our tradespeople are certified. We can provide proof of insurance upon request before any work begins.

What is the Festool dustless sanding system?
+

Festool is a premium German tool brand known for its integrated dust extraction systems. We use Festool sanders connected to dust extractors to capture 99%+ of sanding dust at the source — keeping your home clean during drywall finishing.

Do you offer a warranty?
+

Yes. All our workmanship comes with a 2-year warranty. If anything is not right after we've completed the project, we come back and fix it at no charge.

What payment methods do you accept?
+

We accept e-transfer, cheque, and cash. Payment is structured in milestones — typically 30% upfront, 40% at mid-project, and 30% upon completion.

What areas do you serve?
+

We serve Toronto and all GTA: Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Markham, Oakville, Burlington, Ajax, Whitby, Pickering, Oshawa, and surrounding areas.
